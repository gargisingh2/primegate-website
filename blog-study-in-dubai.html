Studying in Dubai in 2026: Costs, Student Visa and Work Rights

By Tejas Raju, Primegate Consultancy · Last updated 11 August 2026 · 8 min read

Dubai has become one of the world's most talked-about study destinations - tax-free, safe, and packed with global employers. But the two questions families ask most are simple: what does it cost, and can students work? Here is a current, no-nonsense breakdown for 2026.

Key takeaways

  • Total cost: often AED 78,000-156,000/year including living, depending on the programme and lifestyle.
  • Student visa: typically AED 3,000-7,000, usually sponsored by the institution.
  • Work rights: students can work legally in the UAE with a Student Work Permit.
  • Why Dubai: tax-free income, a global business hub, safe and multicultural.

How much does it cost to study in Dubai?

Tuition depends heavily on the level and institution:

Programme levelTypical annual tuition
Foundation / diplomaAED 20,000 - 50,000
Undergraduate degreeAED 30,000 - 90,000
Postgraduate degreeAED 45,000 - 120,000
MBAAED 60,000 - 180,000

Once accommodation, food, transport and personal costs are added, total annual spend commonly lands between AED 78,000 and AED 156,000. Flexible, part-time study options can significantly reduce cost by letting students earn while they learn.

The Dubai student visa - cost and process

International students studying in Dubai are generally sponsored for a student visa by their institution. The visa itself typically costs AED 3,000-7,000, which bundles the application, a mandatory medical test and the Emirates ID. Families should budget a little more once a refundable deposit, health insurance and related items are included. Your institution (or advisor) will guide the exact documents and timing.

Can students work while studying?

Yes - and this is a genuine 2026 shift. UAE rules now allow students to work legally with a Student Work Permit, which has changed the financial maths of studying in Dubai. Eligibility and hours apply, so confirm the current terms with your institution before you rely on it.

Why international students choose Dubai

  • Tax-free income - graduate earnings go further.
  • A global hub for business, finance, technology, logistics and tourism.
  • Safe and multicultural, with English widely spoken.
  • Strong graduate career access in a fast-growing economy.
  • Flexible study options that fit around work.
