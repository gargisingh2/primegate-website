Privacy Policy.

How Primegate Consultancy collects, uses and protects the personal information you share with us.

Last updated 8 September 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Primegate Consultancy ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, shares, and protects the personal information you provide to us - including information you submit through our advertisements and lead forms on LinkedIn and other platforms, and through our website at https://primegateconsultancy.com.

We are committed to handling your personal information responsibly and in accordance with applicable data protection laws, including India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (the "DPDP Act") and applicable data protection law in the United Arab Emirates, where Primegate Consultancy is based.

By submitting your information to us, you confirm that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

2. Who this policy applies to

This policy applies to individuals who interact with Primegate Consultancy through our lead-generation forms, advertisements, website, and other business communications - including study abroad agents, education consultants, and business partners who express interest in working with us.

3. Information we collect

When you submit one of our forms, we may collect:

  • Contact details: your name, email address, and phone number
  • Professional details: your company or agency name and your job title
  • Business information you choose to share: for example, the number of students you place abroad each year, the destination countries you focus on, and your availability to meet our team

We may also receive limited technical information (such as the platform you responded from) from LinkedIn or other advertising services through which you engage with us.

We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under the age of 18.

4. How we collect your information

We collect your information directly from you when you complete a LinkedIn Lead Gen Form, respond to one of our advertisements, contact us, or otherwise choose to share your details with us. Where you submit a form through LinkedIn, LinkedIn may pre-fill certain fields from your profile, which you confirm before submitting.

5. How we use your information

We use your personal information to:

  • Contact you about a potential partnership with Primegate Consultancy
  • Arrange and confirm in-person or virtual meetings with our team
  • Respond to your enquiries and provide relevant information about our services
  • Maintain a record of agents and partners we are in contact with
  • Comply with our legal and regulatory obligations

We will only use your information for the purposes for which it was collected, unless we reasonably consider that we need to use it for a related purpose.

6. Legal basis and consent

We process your personal information on the basis of the consent you provide when you submit your details to us, and where applicable, for our legitimate business interests in establishing and managing partner relationships. You may withdraw your consent at any time (see Section 10).

7. Sharing and disclosure

We may share your information with:

  • Our team members who are responsible for partner relationships and follow-up
  • Service providers who help us operate our business (for example, CRM or communication tools), under appropriate confidentiality obligations
  • Authorities or regulators where required by law

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

8. Cross-border data transfers

Primegate Consultancy works with education institutions and partners located in various countries around the world. Where your information is transferred to or accessed from outside your country of residence, we take reasonable steps to ensure it is handled securely and in line with this Privacy Policy and applicable law.

9. Data retention

We retain your personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes described in this policy, and thereafter for a reasonable period to meet our legal, accounting, or reporting requirements - typically 24 months from your last interaction with us, unless a longer period is required by law. After this, we securely delete or anonymise it.

10. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete information
  • Request deletion of your information
  • Withdraw your consent to our processing at any time
  • Raise a grievance regarding how we handle your data

To exercise any of these rights, or to opt out of further communication from us, contact us using the details in Section 12. We will respond within the timeframe required by applicable law.

11. Data security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your personal information against unauthorised access, loss, misuse, or disclosure. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

12. Contact us / Grievance Officer

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, wish to exercise your rights, or want to raise a concern about how we handle your data, please contact:

  • Grievance Officer, Primegate Consultancy
  • Email: info@primegateconsultancy.com

  • Meydan Grandstand, 6th Floor, Meydan Road, Nad Al Sheba, Dubai, U.A.E

13. Cookies

This website does not currently use cookies or third-party tracking technologies, and we do not run advertising or analytics scripts on it. If this changes, we will update this section and, where required, ask for your consent before any non-essential cookies are set. You can control cookies at any time through your browser settings.

14. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available on this page, with the "Last updated" date revised accordingly.

Questions about your data?

Contact Primegate Consultancy and a member of our team will respond within one working day.
