About the Institution

Malta's Leading Private Higher-Education Institution.

Founded in 2005 and now part of the IDEA Group, IDEA College is a Further and Higher Education Institution licensed by the Malta Further and Higher Education Authority (MFHEA, Licence 2014-FHI-015). It delivers accredited qualifications from MQF Level 4 to MQF Level 8, all aligned with the European Qualifications Framework and recognised across the EU and internationally.

IDEA College sits within a broader ecosystem - alongside IDEA Care and IDEA Consulting - and operates campuses in both Malta and the UAE, a natural bridge for students and partners across the Gulf. Many programmes run part-time, guided by the college's philosophy: "change your life without disrupting your career."

  • MFHEA-licensed Further & Higher Education Institution
  • Qualifications from MQF 4 to MQF 8 (foundation to doctorate)
  • EU / EQF-recognised degrees
  • Campuses in Malta and the UAE
  • Part-time, career-friendly study formats
  • Part of the 20+ year IDEA Group

Why Malta

An English-Speaking EU Education, in the Mediterranean.

Malta offers a rare combination for international students: a full member of the EU and the Schengen area, English as an official language, a safe and sunny environment, and qualifications recognised across Europe and beyond.

For many students it is a more accessible, more affordable route to an EU-recognised degree than the traditional UK or US pathways - with a genuine gateway into the wider European job market.

  • EU member & Schengen area
  • English-speaking country
  • EU-recognised (MQF / EQF) qualifications
  • Safe, Mediterranean lifestyle
  • A gateway to study and work in Europe
