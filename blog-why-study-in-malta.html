Why Malta Is Becoming a Top Study Destination for International Students

By the Primegate Advisory Team · Last updated 13 August 2026 · 4 min read

Key takeaways

  • Malta is an English-speaking EU and Schengen country with qualifications recognised across Europe.
  • Tuition and living costs sit well below the UK or US.
  • It is safe, Mediterranean and compact - an easy first move abroad.
  • It works as a genuine gateway to Europe for further study or work, not a dead end.

A decade ago, few students had Malta on their shortlist. Now it is one of the fastest-rising study destinations we get asked about. The reasons are not glamorous - they are practical, which is exactly why they work.

It is an English-speaking EU country

This is the quiet superpower. English is an official language and the medium of instruction, so there is no language barrier - and yet you are studying inside the EU and Schengen area, with a qualification recognised across Europe. Very few destinations offer both at once.

It costs a fraction of the UK or US

Tuition and living costs in Malta sit well below the traditional English-speaking destinations. For a lot of families we work with, Malta is the difference between a European degree being a nice idea and being genuinely affordable.

It is safe, sunny and small in a good way

Malta consistently ranks as one of the safer places to live, the weather is Mediterranean, and the island is compact enough that students settle in fast. For a first move abroad - often a family's biggest worry - that matters more than any brochure statistic.

"Malta's appeal is almost boringly practical, and that's the point. Parents want their child somewhere safe, in English, with a degree that's respected in Europe, without remortgaging the house. Malta quietly ticks all four. It's not the flashiest option - it's the sensible one, and families are catching on."

— Tejas Raju, Director, Global Business Development & Strategic Partnerships, Primegate Consultancy

It is a genuine gateway to Europe

An EU-recognised qualification, Schengen access, and proximity to the rest of Europe make Malta a stepping stone rather than a dead end - for further study or for work. Students are not choosing Malta instead of Europe; they are choosing it as their way in.

Who it suits

Malta is a strong fit for students who want an affordable, English-language European qualification, career-focused programmes, and a safe first experience of studying abroad. It is where PGC most often places students who want Europe without the UK/US price tag.
