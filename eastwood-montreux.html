About the School

Academic Excellence, Real-World Capability, and Character.

Opened in 2026 on the historic Monte Rosa campus in Montreux, Eastwood Montreux is an international day and boarding school designed for the world students are actually entering - one shaped by AI, fast-changing industries and global challenges.

The school combines world-class academics with structured real-world learning and mentorship, delivered through three interconnected pillars: Knowledge, Action and Character. Project-based learning is developed in partnership with Columbia University's Teachers College, and students learn directly from an international network of leaders across business, science, the arts and public life.

  • Swiss day & boarding school on Lake Geneva
  • IB, Cambridge IGCSE & American Diploma options
  • The IMPACT programme - structured afternoon projects
  • Project-based learning with Columbia's Teachers College
  • Global mentorship network
  • Full boarding house, studios, dining and sports

Why Montreux, Switzerland

Where Swiss Precision Meets International Culture.

Switzerland has long been the home of the world's most respected boarding education - for its safety, its academic heritage, and the global peer networks it creates. Eastwood Montreux sits on the shores of Lake Geneva, minutes from Montreux's cultural venues, sports clubs and transport links.

For families seeking substance, direction and a complete kind of success, it offers an internationally recognised education in one of the world's most trusted settings.

  • On Lake Geneva, Montreux
  • Swiss boarding-school heritage & safety
  • Internationally recognised qualifications
  • A global peer network
  • Historic Monte Rosa campus
