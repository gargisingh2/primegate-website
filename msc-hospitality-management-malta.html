Programme overview

An MQF Level 7 master's for hospitality professionals moving into senior leadership. It combines advanced business management with hospitality practice, covering operations, revenue management, marketing and events, finance, people leadership, health, safety and crisis management, and gastronomy, and ends with a dissertation.

Who it is for

  • Hospitality professionals with supervisory or middle-management experience
  • Event, restaurant, reception, guest services and housekeeping managers

What you will study

  • Tourism and Hospitality Principles
  • Leading and Managing People
  • Hospitality Operations Management
  • Marketing, Sales and Event Management
  • Managing Change in Hospitality
  • Revenue Management in Hospitality
  • The Art and Science of Gastronomy
  • Managing Cultural Diversity in Hospitality
  • Finance for Non-Financial Managers
  • Health, Safety and Crisis Management in Hospitality
  • Dissertation

Entry requirements

  • A Level 6 degree and three years' work experience; or
  • A Level 5 diploma or higher diploma and five years' work experience in a supervisory or managerial role

An advisor will confirm how your qualification maps to these requirements.

Career paths

  • Senior posts such as head of department or events director in hotels, restaurants, casinos and other hospitality organisations

Fees and intakes

Programme fee: €13,500 for the full programme (1.5 years). Intakes run every quarter; your Primegate Consultancy advisor confirms the fee and next start date for your situation.
