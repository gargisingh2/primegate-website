About the Academy

Practical, Accredited Study - Built Around Your Life.

IDEA Academy is a private academy in Dubai, licensed by the Knowledge and Human Development Authority (KHDA, Licence 630698), delivering accredited, industry-driven courses aligned with the European Qualifications Framework (EQF). Part of the wider IDEA Group, it shares the group's philosophy of practical education led by experienced practitioners.

Its hallmark is flexibility: part-time, career-friendly formats that let students upskill and qualify with minimal disruption to their personal and professional lives - a natural fit for Dubai's working professionals and career-changers.

  • KHDA-licensed (Licence 630698)
  • EQF-aligned qualifications (Levels 4-5)
  • Foundation certificates, certificates & diplomas
  • Part-time, flexible, career-friendly study
  • Industry-led, practitioner-taught curriculum
  • Part of the IDEA Group (also in Malta)
