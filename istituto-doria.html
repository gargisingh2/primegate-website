About the Institution

Italian Accreditation, with a Bridge to the Gulf.

Fondazione Doria - Training and Education Institute is an accredited training institute based in the Campania region of Italy. It is accredited by the Campania Region and recognised by the Italian Ministry of Education (MIUR), and holds ISO 9001, ISO 27001 and ISO 45001 certification for quality, information security and workplace safety.

Doria spans vocational, academy and university learning: regional healthcare qualifications, a professional culinary academy, and online university degrees delivered through its partnership with eCampus University. Alongside its Italian programmes it runs an office in Dubai, making its qualifications and pathways accessible to students across the UAE, the wider Gulf and South Asia.
