What Happens After You Submit.

1

Personal Review

Your enquiry is read personally - not processed by an automated system. A member of the founding team reads every incoming contact.

2

A Considered Response

You will receive a response within one working day - with a proposed time for either a structured partnership call (institutional enquiries) or an initial advisory conversation (student and family enquiries).

3

The Conversation

The initial conversation is obligation-free. It is a chance for both sides to assess genuine alignment. If Primegate is not the right fit, we will say so honestly and suggest alternatives.
