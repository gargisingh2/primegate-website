About this stream

The fashion, beauty and personal care stream covers tailoring, clothing manufacturing, beauty and aesthetics, and hairdressing and styling. Practical training uses working salon stations and professional equipment.

  • Artisan tailoring and clothing manufacturing
  • Professional beautician and aesthetics
  • Professional hairdressing and styling

Your journey to Italy

  1. Choose your programme and receive initial counselling from your Primegate Consultancy advisor.
  2. Submit your application and supporting documents; Fondazione Doria reviews the application.
  3. Receive your offer letter and complete the fee payment.
  4. Get assistance with the visa process and pre-departure requirements.
  5. Travel to Italy, begin your programme and progress toward the professional internship pathway.
