What the Partnership Gives You

Five Reasons This Partnership Is Different From the Rest.

Primegate's university partnerships have been built over thirty years of personal engagement - with international recruitment directors, regional coordinators, and admissions offices that trust the Primegate name before a student application arrives. As a channel partner, you carry that institutional credibility into every student conversation. That is an access advantage no competitor network can match in this region.

Primegate operates on a fully disclosed commission model - competitive with the market, paid promptly upon enrolment confirmation, and never contingent on volume targets that compromise advisory quality. We believe agents who are paid fairly advise students better. Our commission structure reflects that belief.

The Primegate partner onboarding programme is not a product catalogue. It is an immersion into the institutional knowledge, market intelligence, and advisory methodology that has produced results for three decades. Partner agents leave onboarding knowing more about the universities they represent - their admissions culture, cohort priorities, and scholarship frameworks - than most agent networks learn in years.

In the education advisory market, brand association matters. Families that recognise the Primegate name bring a level of trust and seriousness to the advisory relationship that generic agent interactions rarely command. Our partners benefit from that brand equity - in every meeting, every referral, and every application submitted under the Primegate umbrella.

The ethical and regulatory landscape of international student recruitment is increasingly scrutinised - by universities, regulators, and families. Primegate's compliance framework, documentation standards, and institutional relationship protocols are designed to ensure that every partner operates within the highest ethical standards. This protects your business, your reputation, and the students you serve.

How It Works

Three Steps From Application to Active Partnership.

1

Apply

Complete the online partner application at the top of this page and upload your business licence. We review every application personally - assessing your advisory background, current student base, source markets served, and alignment with the Primegate standard. Primegate is selective about who joins the network. This is by design.

2

Onboard & Get Certified

Accepted partners complete the Primegate Partner Onboarding Programme - a structured introduction to the institutions you will represent, the student profiles Primegate matches, the compliance standards we operate to, and the advisory methodology that underpins every placement. This is the foundation of the partnership.

3

Start Placing Students

With onboarding complete, you begin referring qualified students from your network to Primegate's institutional partners. Primegate handles the institutional relationship, application infrastructure, compliance documentation, and post-acceptance coordination. You focus on your students. We manage the institutional side.

The Partner Toolkit

What You Get.

Direct University Access

A curated portfolio of university and institutional partnerships across the UK, USA, Canada, Australia, Europe, and the UAE. Not a directory. Not an aggregator listing. A selective set of relationships earned through thirty years of direct engagement - now accessible to partners who meet the Primegate standard.

Co-Branded Marketing Materials

Partners receive co-branded collateral - digital and print - carrying both the partner's identity and the Primegate endorsement. This dual branding signals to prospective families that the agent operates within an established, credentialed network. Materials are updated to reflect portfolio changes and scholarship cycles.

Dedicated Partner Support Contact

Every Primegate partner is assigned a named support contact - not a helpdesk ticket queue. Your contact has direct access to the institutional relationships, application infrastructure, and advisory knowledge base. When a complex case requires expert input, that input is accessible within one working day.

Transparent Commission Payments

Commission rates are disclosed at onboarding and confirmed in the partner agreement. Payments are processed on a fixed post-enrolment schedule - no ambiguity, no delays tied to institutional payment cycles. Partners receive full visibility into referral status, application progression, and commission pipeline.

Eligibility & Selectivity

Who Primegate Chooses to Partner With.

The Primegate partner network is not open registration. Every partner is reviewed, assessed, and onboarded individually - because the quality of every student Primegate places is a direct function of the quality of the advisory that precedes the placement.

We are looking for agents and consultancies that share our belief in honest, student-centred advising - not operators who are primarily interested in volume.

  • A minimum of two years of active education advisory practice, or equivalent professional background
  • An established student base or referral network in at least one core source market (GCC, South Asia, SEA, CIS, Africa)
  • A demonstrable understanding of the international higher education application process for at least one destination market
  • No documented history of non-compliance, misrepresentation, or regulatory sanction
  • Genuine alignment with Primegate's values: student-first guidance, full transparency, and ethical commission practices
"We are not looking for the largest network. We are looking for the right one. If you advise students with the same standard you would want applied to your own children's education, we would like to talk."

Questions From Prospective Partners.

Commission rates are competitive with market standards and vary by institution and programme level. Rates are fully disclosed at onboarding and confirmed in writing in the partner agreement before any referrals are made. We do not advertise specific rates publicly - contact us to discuss.

No exclusivity is required. Partners are free to maintain existing university relationships alongside the Primegate portfolio. However, we do require that partner agents do not simultaneously represent competing advisory networks that conflict with Primegate's compliance standards.

Primegate requires a minimum of two years of relevant advisory or professional experience. If you are in the earlier stages of building a practice, we recommend establishing your student base first and returning when you have a track record to present.

The onboarding programme covers: institutional portfolio overview (each partner university in detail), student profile matching methodology, application submission standards, compliance and ethics framework, commission and payment process, and use of co-branded marketing materials. Most partners complete it within two to three weeks.

The student relationship is owned by the partner agent. Primegate provides the institutional access, application infrastructure, and compliance support. The advisory relationship with the student - counselling, shortlisting, family management - belongs to the partner. This division is explicit and protected in the partner agreement.

Primegate reviews all complete partner applications within 3-4 working days. You will receive either an invitation to proceed to an introductory call or, if the application does not meet current criteria, a clear explanation of why and what would be required to reapply.

No. There is no membership fee, joining cost, or subscription charge. Primegate's revenue derives from the institutional commissions associated with successful placements. Partner agents earn their share of that commission upon student enrolment.

Primegate partners are issued a co-branded marketing pack that includes approved materials for use in the partner's own collateral. Direct use of the Primegate logo without authorisation is not permitted. The partner pack and brand usage guidelines are provided at onboarding.
