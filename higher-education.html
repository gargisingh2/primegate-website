Why PGC

Why Work With an International Education Consultancy.

Primegate Consultancy (PGC) has spent thirty years building direct relationships with universities, colleges and awarding bodies - the foundation of any dependable study abroad consultancy. As an overseas education consultancy, PGC doesn't just advise; for institutions like IDEA College, Malta and IDEA Academy, UAE, PGC is the appointed Exclusive Representative Office, meaning applications, admissions guidance and enrolment support all run through a single accountable partner. Every institution PGC represents is independently accredited - through recognised national and international bodies including Malta's MFHEA and Dubai's KHDA - so students, agents and universities alike are working with a consultancy built on verified, recognised partnerships rather than a directory of listings.
